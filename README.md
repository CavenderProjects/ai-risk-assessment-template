# AI Risk Assessment Template

> **In active development, Q3 2026**

A practitioner-built framework for assessing AI risk in regulated environments. Maps NIST AI RMF 1.0 + ISO/IEC 42001 controls to GRC language enterprises already use, so compliance teams, auditors, and risk managers can evaluate AI systems without first becoming AI engineers.

---

## The Problem

AI governance frameworks exist. The translation layer between framework language and operational GRC practice doesn't.

Auditors don't speak NIST AI RMF natively. Risk officers don't know which AI RMF control maps to their existing ISO 27001 program. Regulated-environment practitioners need to assess AI systems using the frameworks they already know, not learn a new discipline from scratch to answer an auditor's question.

This template does that translation.

---

## What's Being Built

- **Model risk translation table:** NIST AI RMF GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE functions mapped to ISO/IEC 42001 controls and enterprise GRC language
- **AI risk assessment template:** filled example in audit-ready format, covering training data provenance, drift monitoring, edge case scope, and accountability ownership
- **AI vendor risk questionnaire:** 25-question due diligence framework addressing the gap left by pre-2023 vendor contracts with no AI clause
- **Regulatory exposure mapping:** AI deployment scenarios mapped to HIPAA, NYDFS, GDPR, CPRA, EU AI Act, PCI-DSS
